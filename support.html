03

Care guide

Hardwax oil and wool both age rather than wear out, as long as you give them a little attention once a year.

Oiled timber
Re-oil once a year, cloth not brush
Marks in oil
Sand lightly with 320 grit, re-oil the panel
Wool bouclé
Brush along the loop, never pull a snag
Velvet
Steam from 10 cm to lift the pile
Leather
Condition twice a year, keep out of direct sun
Unfinished teak
Leave it; it silvers evenly in two seasons

Every chair carries a ten-year structural guarantee. Frames, joints and springs are covered; fabric wear and sun fade are not.

04

Questions

Can I order in my own fabric?

Yes. Send a metre to the workshop and we will tell you whether it will hold up on that frame before anything is cut. Allow two extra weeks.

Will two chairs match?

Close, not identical. Boards are matched for grain within a single order, but timber is not printed, so colour varies a little between pieces and will even out as they age.

Do you ship outside Nigeria?

Yes, to most of West Africa and to Europe. Freight is quoted per order once we know the destination; delivery into the room is included where the courier allows it.

Can I change an order after placing it?

Fabric and finish can change up to the point the frame is cut, usually about ten days in. After that we can still change the cover, but the frame is set.

What if it does not fit through the door?

Tell us the narrowest point before you order and we will check it against the piece. Legs come off most chairs; sofa backs do not, so the measurement matters.

Do you repair older pieces?

Always, including ones outside the guarantee. Re-webbing, re-oiling and re-covering are all done in the same workshop that built the piece.
