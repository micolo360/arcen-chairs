Our story

We started with one chair and a stubborn question

What would a chair look like if nothing on it was there by habit? Arcen began in a workshop in 2019 with that question, a lathe, and a plank of walnut that had been drying for eleven years. The pedestal chair that came out of it is still in the collection, unchanged.

More Than a Chair.

A chair can fill a space.

The right chair can define it.

We create furniture for people who appreciate thoughtful design, exceptional craftsmanship, and the beauty of simplicity.

From the first sketch to the final stitch, every detail exists for a reason.

How a chair gets made

Four weeks on the bench

Step 01

Drawing

Full-size on paper first, then a seat mock-up in foam. If the curve of the back cannot be felt in thirty seconds, it goes back to the board.

Step 02

Frame

Boards are matched for grain, turned on the lathe, and left to settle for a fortnight before the joints are cut. Nothing is veneered.

Step 03

Upholstery

Latex and wool are layered by hand over a webbed base, then covered and stitched in a single piece so the seam follows the seat line.

Step 04

Finish

Three coats of hardwax oil, burnished between each. Brass feet are fitted last, by the person who built the frame, who signs underneath.

Every material is selected not only for how it looks, but for how it feels, ages, and lives with you.
