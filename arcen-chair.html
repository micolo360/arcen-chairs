About this piece

A turned walnut pedestal, a floating velvet backrest and a deep floral seat. It was the first chair we made and it still sets the brief for every one that follows.

The pedestal is turned from one piece of walnut, which is why the grain runs unbroken around it rather than meeting at a seam. The backrest is held on two slim posts so it floats clear of the seat and flexes a little when you lean back — the only part of the chair that is allowed to move.
